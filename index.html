Return Policy

We want you to be satisfied with your purchase. If you wish to return an eligible product, please contact SmartStore after receiving your order.

Return Charges

If the price of the returned product is above ₹100, a return charge of ₹30 will be deducted from the refundable amount.

The remaining eligible amount will be processed as the refund.

Return Conditions

  • The product should be in acceptable condition.
  • The product should be returned with original accessories where applicable.
  • Return requests may be reviewed before approval.

How to Request a Return

Contact SmartStore with your Order ID and relevant order details to request a return.
